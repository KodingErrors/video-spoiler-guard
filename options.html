Video Spoiler Guard

Adds a random amount of fake time to the length video players show, so you can't tell how close the end is.

Auto-detect

Fake time

Each video gets its own random amount in this range, and keeps it every time you come back to it.

Sites

Sites that are on are always guarded; with auto-detect on, they also show the dialog unless you untick it for that site. Sites that are off are never changed. Each entry also covers its subdomains.

    What it can't hide

    • The position of the progress bar still reflects the real length unless you hide it.
    • Lengths printed on thumbnails, in search results and in chapter lists.
    • Players that draw their controls in a canvas or in the browser's built-in controls.
    • Disney+: its player doesn't report the video's length to the page, so there is nothing to work from.
